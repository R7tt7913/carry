# 📂 carry - Sync your files between trusted devices

[Visit the website to download carry](https://github.com/R7tt7913/carry)

Carry helps you sync project folders across your own computers. It keeps your files current and accessible on every device you use. The app works on Windows and focuses on the security of your data.

## 📥 How to install carry

Follow these steps to get the app running on your computer.

1. Go to the [official download page](https://github.com/R7tt7913/carry).
2. Look for the "Releases" section on the right side of your screen.
3. Select the latest version listed at the top.
4. Click the file ending in `.msi` to start the download.
5. Open the downloaded file once complete.
6. Follow the instructions on the screen to finish the installation.

## 🛠️ How it works

Carry creates a secure bridge between your computers. It uses encryption to protect your information while it moves over the network. You control which folders you sync and which devices have access to those folders.

## ✨ Main features

- **Automatic sync:** Changes you make in one folder appear on your other devices.
- **Privacy focus:** You own your data. Carry does not upload your files to a central cloud server.
- **Fast transfer:** The app moves files directly between your connected devices.
- **AI memory support:** Keep your AI agent settings and memory files in sync for a consistent experience across machines.
- **Encryption:** Carry protects your data during transit to ensure only your trusted devices see your files.

## 🖥️ System requirements

- Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11.
- Network: A stable internet connection.
- Storage: 200 MB of free disk space for the app files.

## 🔒 Security

Privacy remains the core goal of this app. Because it uses end-to-end encryption, your data is readable only by the devices you link yourself. You manage your keys locally and no third party can access your information.

## 📂 Managing folders

Open the app after installation to start the setup process. You will see a list of your connected devices on the main dashboard. Click "Add Folder" to choose a directory on your computer for synchronization. Select the target devices you want to include in this sync group. Once you confirm, the app will scan the files and match them across your chosen machines.

## ⚙️ Settings

You can adjust how the app behaves in the settings menu. You might choose to start the app when Windows logs in or limit the speed of file transfers to save bandwidth. Updates happen automatically, so you always have the latest improvements for stability and security.

## 💬 Getting help

If you encounter issues, check the issues tab on the website. You can look for existing problems that match yours or create a new report. Provide details about what you did and where the process stopped so others can help you fix it.

## 📦 What to expect

Carry works in the background of your system. You will see a small icon in your taskbar near the clock. This icon shows the status of your connections. A green light means your devices are synced and ready. A yellow light means the app is currently moving files. If you need to pause syncing, right-click the icon and choose "Pause Sync."

## 💾 Regular updates

The software receives updates to keep it compatible with new versions of Windows. Check the website occasionally to see if new features appear. Your settings will persist even after you update your version of the app.

## 🗝️ Privacy and trust

You are the only person with access to your sync settings. Because the app does not rely on a central database of users, your activity remains private to you. Trust is built on the fact that your files never leave your direct control during the transfer process.
